Background

Why

This sample is an early interface idea, not completed project work. Its purpose is to provide a structure for a future case study.

Problem

How might a local discovery interface make it easier to explore nearby?

A framing question to explore, rather than a finding from research.

Starting Point

Research questions

These are proposed areas for investigation. They have not been tested or validated.

  • What information helps someone decide whether to explore a place?
  • How could an interface make local discovery approachable?
  • What access information would need to be verified?

Proposed direction

One possible direction pairs clear categories with concise place information. These ideas are proposals, rather than validated findings.

Design Iterations

Proposed sequence

  1. Explore nearby
  2. Find a possibility
  3. Learn more

Illustrative sequence, not validated.

Design Rationale

Stay close

Keep nearby possibilities easy to browse.

Offer useful context

Leave space for practical, verified details.

Make room for everyone

Explore ways to communicate access information clearly.

Product

The illustrative screen uses neighborhood-inspired shapes and simple place cards. Listings are placeholders and do not represent real businesses or events.
