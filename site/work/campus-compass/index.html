01 / OVERVIEW

A starting point for possibility.

This sample starts with a question about finding resources on campus. It does not assume that students have a particular problem; that would need to be established through research.

02 / THE QUESTION

How might a campus resource finder make the first step feel easier?

A framing question to explore, rather than a finding from research.

03 / EXPLORATION

Questions before answers.

These are proposed areas for investigation. They have not been tested or validated.

  1. What do students look for when they need a resource?
  2. Which details help someone decide where to go?
  3. How can a resource finder support different access needs?

A proposed flow starts with a broad category, narrows the options, and leaves space for practical information. These are early design possibilities to explore, rather than validated decisions.
