EVERYDAY RITUALS / CONCEPT

01 / OVERVIEW

A starting point for possibility.

This placeholder explores the visual and interaction possibilities of a reflective daily interface. It makes no claims about mental-health benefits, habits, or user needs.

02 / THE QUESTION

How might a daily interface leave room to pause?

A framing question to explore, rather than a finding from research.

03 / EXPLORATION

Questions before answers.

These are proposed areas for investigation. They have not been tested or validated.

  1. What would make a check-in feel optional and approachable?
  2. How much input feels appropriate for a brief interaction?
  3. How could the interface respect someone’s privacy?

A proposed sequence offers a simple prompt, a small choice, and a short response. The visual direction uses warm neutrals and open space to explore a quieter experience.

01

Keep the invitation small

Begin with one clear question instead of a long form.

02

Make space for choice

Treat participation as optional, without scores or streaks.

03

Respect the moment

Keep demo responses temporary and avoid collecting personal information.

An idea made tangible.
