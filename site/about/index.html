About Me

I’m an undergraduate at the University of Michigan pursuing a BA in Art & Design and a BSI in User Experience Design.

I started in fine arts, working in drawing and painting, but wanted to explore how I could use my creativity to solve problems alongside people with different perspectives. That led me to graphic and UI/UX design, where I can help make everyday experiences more enjoyable, inclusive, and intuitive. My process combines strategic thinking, color psychology, and visual storytelling to bring that goal to life.

Campus Involvements

  • Atlas Digital

    • Product Designer

    Atlas Digital is a student organization at the University of Michigan building technology and design solutions for businesses, startups, and nonprofits. As a Product Designer, I help turn client needs into approachable digital experiences that support their goals.

  • The Michigan Daily (Web Team)

    • UI/UX Designer

    The Michigan Daily is a student-run news organization independent of the university. On the Web Team’s product subteam, I help design apps and website features, collaborating with engineers and teams across the organization from research to implementation.

  • MA:E Magazine

    • Layout Designer
    • Assistant Design Director

    MA:E Magazine is a student publication and creative collective at the University of Michigan centered on the APIDA community. Among its multiple teams, I’m on the creative team’s design subteam, creating the final published layouts for editorial and photoshoot spreads.
