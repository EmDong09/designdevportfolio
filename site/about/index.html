About Me

Hi, I’m Emily — exploring where thoughtful experiences meet visual expression.

I’m studying User Experience Design + Art and Design at the University of Michigan. I’m interested in how useful digital experiences and expressive visual design can work together.

My process moves between asking questions, sketching possibilities, and making ideas tangible. I’m still exploring the kinds of problems and creative practices I want to grow into.

Campus Involvements

  • Atlas Digital

    • Product Designer

    Atlas Digital is a student organization at the University of Michigan building technology and design solutions for businesses, startups, and nonprofits. As a Product Designer, I help turn client needs into approachable digital experiences that support their goals and communities.

  • The Michigan Daily (Web Team)

  • MA:E Magazine

    • Publication Layout Designer
    • Assistant Design Director

    MA:E Magazine is a student publication and creative collective at the University of Michigan centered on the APIDA community. Among its multiple teams, I’m on the creative team’s design subteam, creating the final published layouts for editorial and photoshoot spreads.
